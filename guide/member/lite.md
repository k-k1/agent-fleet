# Simple guide (for people who don't use the terminal)

English | [日本語](lite.ja.md)

The entry point of this volume (what to read first, and the one rule to keep) has moved to
[Start here if you don't use the terminal](../user/start/without-terminal.md). What you can do is in
[What you can do with Agent Fleet](../user/start/overview.md), the screen in [Find your way around the screen](../user/start/console-tour.md),
and the first-time setup in [Start your first session](../user/start/quickstart.md). The chapters below have not moved yet.

---

## 4. Using chat (questions, translation, text cleanup)

From **Assistant** on the left you can ask the AI questions and have it translate or summarize. How to start and when to use a session instead is in [Ask an assistant](../user/chat/assistant.md); translating files and purpose-built assistants are in [Use translation and purpose-built assistants](../user/chat/purpose-assistants.md).

---

## 5. Reading documents and slides

Documents written by the dev team open from **Files** on the left: walk down the folders and click a file name. How
Markdown, slides, diagrams and PDFs are shown is in [View documents and diagrams](../user/files/view.md); asking an
assistant to translate or summarise a file is in [Hand a file to a session](../user/files/send.md).

---

## 6. The memo queue (collect ideas and hand them over)

Collect requests as they come in the **Memo queue** on the left, and hand them to a session later. How it works, and who receives your memos, is in [Collect memos and hand them over](../user/chat/memos.md).

---

## 7. Watching development progress

The **Sessions** list on the left shows what state each piece of development AI work is in. How to read the state marks
and follow a conversation is in [Read a session's state](../user/sessions/status.md).

A conversation a developer shared with you is under **Shared sessions** on the left, read-only. How to read it is in [Share a conversation](../user/team/share.md#read-a-conversation-shared-with-you).

### An important ground rule

The rule not to answer questions meant for someone else has moved to [Start here if you don't use the terminal](../user/start/without-terminal.md#dont-answer-questions-meant-for-someone-else).

---

## 8. When you're stuck + FAQ

What to try first when something goes wrong, and the FAQ, are in [Read the frequently asked questions](../user/troubleshooting/faq.md).

When you feel like editing files yourself or having the AI write code, the developer-facing [Using Agent Fleet](README.md) is your next step.

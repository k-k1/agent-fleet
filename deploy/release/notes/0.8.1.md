Signing in is no longer tied to Google — any OpenID Connect provider can be enabled,
and a person keeps one workspace across an email change or a second provider. The
Console also stops freezing for seconds at a time in Chrome.

### New

- **Sign in with any OpenID Connect provider, not just Google.** Enable Entra ID, Okta,
  Keycloak, Auth0, Cognito or GitLab by listing it in `AF_OIDC_PROVIDERS` with an
  issuer, a client ID and a secret; the sign-in screen shows one button per provider,
  and several can run side by side. **Google needs no change.** An incompletely
  configured provider is disabled with a warning rather than taking everyone else's
  sign-in down. A Microsoft issuer left on `common`/`organizations` without a tenant
  allowlist is refused at startup — it would put every holder of a Microsoft account at
  your front door.
- **One person keeps one workspace across an email change or a second provider.** Who
  you are is now recorded against the provider's own immutable account id rather than
  your email address, so a surname change or a domain merger no longer grows a second,
  empty workspace. A sign-in whose email matches nobody deliberately becomes a **new**
  person, and says so right after sign-in.
- **A Claude session tells you its login has expired.** The badge reads **login expired
  — sign in again**, the connection card shows the date instead of claiming you are
  still connected, and sending a prompt is refused with the reason. Until now the only
  sign was that nothing ever started. Keys still reach the pane, so `/login` in the
  terminal recovers.

### Fixed

- **The Console froze for seconds at a time in Chrome.** Panes were rebuilding healthy
  GPU contexts until the browser evicted one a visible terminal was still using, and
  the fallback renderer measured every distinct character through the page layout — on
  a Japanese screen, thousands of them. Terminals now fall back to a canvas renderer,
  and panes no longer evict one another. Firefox and touch devices were unaffected.
- **"Cannot reach the workspace agent" when the agent had in fact answered** — opening
  a terminal for a stopped session reported a connection failure instead of the real
  answer, that there was no session running and no history left to show.
- **Free text typed into a Claude question was thrown away** when the question offered
  previews for its options; your text is now sent as a note against it. A question you
  dismissed also carries a **dismissed** badge instead of being rendered as answered.
- **Every shared-session tab was labelled "Shared session"** — tabs now carry the
  session's own name and its agent's icon.

### Upgrade notes

Nothing to migrate. The table that records which provider account you signed in with is
added at start, holds no data from before the upgrade, and is ignored by an older build,
so a rollback is safe; home directories do not move. Nothing about sign-in changes until
you set `AF_OIDC_PROVIDERS`. The pinned agent CLI versions are unchanged from 0.8.0.

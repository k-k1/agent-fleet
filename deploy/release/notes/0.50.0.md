Spaces give work that belongs to no repository a folder of its own, started from a reorganised
Start screen, and shells and SSM sessions get a Terminals view. A new Active view lists what is going
on now, the launch dialog picks agent, route and model in one strip under the first prompt, and
Web Push can be switched off per origin.

### New

- **Spaces:** "Start in a space" on the Start screen starts a session in a new or existing folder
  under `~/spaces`, for work that is not a repository. Spaces are listed in their own section of
  the Sessions view, can be renamed, and are deleted through the trash together with their
  sessions; files attached to a session in a space are copied into its `attachments` folder.
  ([#4963](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4963),
  [#4965](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4965))
- **Start screen:** three sections, "Start in a space", "Start in a repository" and "Other", one open
  at a time, sharing one draft of the first prompt. "Start working" now asks what first, then
  where (Location), then the rest under Details.
  ([#4963](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4963))
- **Terminals view:** a view on the activity bar lists shells by place (home, repository, space)
  and SSM sessions by host, and its **+** opens "Start a terminal".
  ([#4966](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4966),
  [#5169](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5169))
- **Active view:** a view on the activity bar lists the sessions running now and the stopped ones
  still waiting for you, as cards grouped by repository and space, with child sessions folded under
  their parent. Auto-run and schedule runs sit in two groups of their own that start folded.
  ([#5154](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5154),
  [#5165](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5165))
- **Activity-bar icons:** right-clicking (or long-pressing) the activity bar turns each view's
  icon on or off, and the choice follows you across browsers. A hidden view that has something
  waiting shows an amber dot. ([#4964](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4964))
- **Run-with strip:** in the launch dialog and the space form, the agent, route and model are
  chosen in a strip under the first prompt, with your recently used models one click away, and the
  launch button names what it starts. ([#5146](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5146))
- **Memo auto-run, run next:** a waiting memo can be marked "Run next" from its menu, so the next
  free slot takes it before older memos; `add_memo` can set the mark when it adds a memo to a queue.
  ([#5021](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5021))
- **Schedules, "Also show in the Sessions view":** with this set on a schedule, the sessions its
  runs start in their own worktree are listed in the repository tree and the Sessions view as yours.
  ([#5070](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5070))
- **Web Push by origin:** ⚙ Settings → Notifications can switch off pushes from auto-run or
  schedule sessions, or from the sessions they start, and each push's title says where it came from.
  ([#5081](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5081))
- **Memory, promote to project documents:** Import & export → Maintenance lists the memories that
  have proven themselves, proposes the change to the repository's own documents, and retires the
  memory once that change is merged. The lint report checks such document targets and offers to
  re-point or bring the memory back. ([#3498](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/3498))
- **Boost budget:** a tenant's monthly boost budget is now enforced: a boost past it is refused.
  The tenant's Boost card lists each member's boosted hours this month, and the member detail shows
  them. ([#4417](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4417))

### Improved

- **Auto-run view:** a filter box at the top finds runs by category, work item, session or branch.
  ([#5115](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5115))
- **Forms:** explanations that used to sit under fields in the launch forms, the auto-run, schedule
  and other dialogs, and tenant settings are behind an ⓘ beside the label; warnings stay in view.
  ([#5147](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5147),
  [#5148](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5148))
- **Toolchains:** rows show the product name linked to its upstream page, and gitleaks and the
  Session Manager plugin are listed. ([#5072](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5072),
  [#5068](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5068))
- **Memo auto-run:** reading a run's last answer (for the `Done:` rule) reaches further back in long
  sessions and no longer stalls on members whose workspace is stopped.
  ([#4891](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4891))
- **Workspace image:** OpenCode 2.4 is supported, and Chromium, Go, the AWS CLI, gcloud, bkt, the
  Confluence CLI and the bundled MCP servers are updated.
  ([#4982](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4982),
  [#5180](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5180),
  [#5181](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5181))

### Fixed

- **Custom API endpoint:** a Managed session could not start when the approved custom endpoint was
  the workspace's only provider. ([#4789](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4789))
- **Memos:** a refused save closed the editor and lost the draft, a retried send could send earlier
  batches again, an image pasted just before a memo delete could lose its file, and the memo picker
  could scroll the pointed memo out of view.
  ([#4939](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4939),
  [#4830](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4830),
  [#4827](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4827),
  [#4943](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4943))
- **Schedules:** "Review & approve" right after a refused approval reopened the outdated copy, and a
  "Finished" fold could reopen after the section was closed.
  ([#4856](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4856),
  [#4762](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4762))
- **Web Push:** tapping a push from another tenant opened the session in the current one, a device's
  switch could show the wrong state after a sign-out or a re-registration, and pushes for a session
  could stay held back after you had left it. ([#4870](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4870),
  [#4841](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4841),
  [#4866](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4866))
- **Notifications:** one oversized or malformed notification could hold back the ones after it.
  ([#4862](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4862),
  [#4863](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4863))
- **Secret masking:** text glued right after a masked token stayed in clear, and some short GitHub
  `ghs_` tokens were not recognised. ([#4945](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4945),
  [#4948](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4948))
- **Console:** the memory consolidator's "Run now" could write to another tenant after a switch
  during its confirmation; a removed member could reappear on the member detail; and the browser
  offered saved addresses in free-text fields.
  ([#4925](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4925),
  [#4903](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4903),
  [#5123](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/5123))
- **ECS teardown:** without `--purge-secrets`, a teardown still deleted the Control Plane's secrets
  when its `SsmPrefix` was under `/af-ws/`. ([#4894](https://github.com/k-k1/agent-fleet/issues/4894))

### Upgrade notes

- **Boost budgets are now enforced:** a tenant that set a monthly boost budget will see boosts
  refused once a member's boosted hours reach it. Review the budget on the tenant's Boost card.
- **Spaces need the updated workspace:** until a workspace runs 0.50.0, the Start screen says its Agent
  has no spaces and does not launch there.

One fix for administrators: you can now take yourself off a tenant's roster, as long as
it is not the last roster you are on.

### Fixed

- **You could not remove your own membership from any tenant, even while you were a
  member of several.** The roster refused every self-removal to protect the one thing
  with no undo inside the product — losing your own way back in — but that is a
  property of your *last* membership, not of any of them. It now refuses only when the
  row you are removing is the only active membership you have left. This bit hardest
  where the product's own procedure puts you: a golden snapshot has to be seeded from
  an account that can sign in, so you make a throwaway tenant with your own account,
  and a deployment with a single administrator could not then clean it up.

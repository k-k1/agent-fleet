One fix for administrators: you can now take yourself off a tenant's roster, as long as
it is not the last roster you are on.

### Fixed

- **You could not remove your own membership from any tenant, even while you were a
  member of several.** The roster refused every self-removal to protect the one thing
  with no undo inside the product — losing your own way back in — but that is a property
  of your *last* membership, not of any of them. It now refuses only when the row you are
  removing is the only active membership you have left. Where this bit hardest is a place
  the product's own procedure puts you: the seed for a golden snapshot has to be an
  account that can sign in, so you make a throwaway tenant with your own account — and
  then a deployment with a single administrator could not clean it up. There is nobody
  else to ask, tenants cannot be deleted, and destroying a workspace requires the
  membership to be off the roster first. On `ecs-ec2` the slot that throwaway home holds
  also blocks the automatic golden bake, which will not begin with fewer than two free
  slots.
